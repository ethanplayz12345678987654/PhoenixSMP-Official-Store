Staff

Meet the people who keep Stasis Network running. We're a small, dedicated team focused on community, stability, and fairness.

Roles

Executives

The backend and the top ranks of the network. We are the Founder, Co-Founder, and Co-Owner.

Management Teams

Top-level leadership and decision makers for the network. We are the Director, Manager, and The Executives.

Administrative Team

Leaders of moderating and making the server a better place. We are the Admins, and The Management Team.

Moderation Team

Server moderators and the server's security and chat filter. We are the Moderators and The Administrative Team.

Development Team

We are the developers of the server. We are the Developers and The Founder.

Director

Responsible for all community moderation, rule enforcement, and support. They can also supervise and direct all of staff. Though a bit different, they still have the same amount of power.

  • Server Director1 — In-game moderation
  • Staff Director2 — Community manager

Managers

Responsible for most community moderation, rule enforcement, and support. They can supervise staff, interview people, and act as admins.

  • Moderator1 — In-game moderation
  • Moderator2 — Community manager

Administrators

Higher ranked Moderator but with extra permissions.

  • Admin 1 — Server admin
  • Admin 2 - Server admin

Moderators

Responsible for community moderation, rule enforcement, and support.

  • Moderator1 — In-game moderation
  • Moderator2 — Community manager

Developers

Plugin & server development, feature work, and tooling.

  • Dev1 — Backend and scripts
  • Dev2 — Website and integrations

Helpers

Responsible for slight community moderation, rule enforcement, and support. This is the lowest rank of moderation and where people will get promoted to higher ranks!

  • Helper1 — In-game moderation
  • Helper2 — Community moderation

Advisors

Help the Owners with ideas and thoughts from the public. They are not part of staff but can be.

  • Advisor1 — Giving Public Thoughts
  • Advisor2 — Giving Public Thoughts
